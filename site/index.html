The Science

The thesis is
peer-reviewed.

Nature Human Behaviour · Feb 2, 2026
Proven

A peer-reviewed study in Nature Human Behaviour found that large language models predict personality traits more accurately than close friends and family from open-ended narrative — from text alone. ConversaTrait was already built on that premise when it published.

Internal Pilot Study
r = 0.83

Correlation with a clinical Big Five Inventory baseline across a small internal cohort. Non-IRB, anecdotal at best — but a strong signal to justify a fully funded, third-party, university-paired replication. That is the next step.

The Input
Any conversation.
Any platform.
Any written text.

WhatsApp, Discord, email, SMS, interview transcripts. If it is text, it is data. No surveys. No self-report bias. No 200-question form nobody finishes.

The Model Gap
Model-agnostic.
200+ models.

The Nature study used GPT-4.1. ConversaTrait runs on any model you choose — over 200 of them — where most tools give you no choice at all. The accuracy delta between the study model and current-generation models is staggering.
